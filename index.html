Hold the Keep

A siege in twenty waves

Something opened a portal at the east end of the valley, and it is not picky about what it sends through. Goblins first. Then the dead, the things from the stars, the machines. The keep's walls have 1,000 hit points and no one is coming to help.

  • Recruit defenders with gold. They march out the gate and fight on their own. Every kind of defender is yours from the first wave.
  • Take control of any soldier with C or by clicking them, and fight with WASD.
  • Shieldmen and Paladins hold the front. Blades and cavalry kill what they hold.
  • Archers, crossbows, bombers, mages and the ballista strike from behind.
  • Healers follow the army and mend the wounded.

Every monster you meet is recorded in the Bestiary with its health, attack, rarity and story. Until you have seen one, it is only a shadow. Keys recruit, drag pans, scroll zooms, P pauses, B opens the Bestiary.

Or lead the horde. Take the portal's side: spend souls on any of the 50 monsters, gather them at the portal, and press Charge to send them all at once while the keep pours out reinforcements every 28 seconds. Break 3,500 points of wall before the twentieth reinforcement, or the King's army arrives and the siege is lost.

Or rule a lair. The same world from the other side: a warlord with a name, a hole in the hills, goblin kin instead of villagers, meat instead of grain, and a horde bred from the Spawning Pit. Every human ruler on the map will come for you sooner or later.

Or rule a country. Log in as a ruler with a name, pick any of the world's countries on the map or found your own, and run a city: farms, houses, villagers trained into soldiers with names of their own. Hunt monsters to level up and unlock guns and siege engines. Other rulers will declare war on you. Keep everyone fed, because hunger kills rulers too.
